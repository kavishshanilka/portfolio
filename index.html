Semi-Finalist · Spark Challenge 2025–2026

GridGuard — Autonomous Edge-Coordinated Energy Management System

Distributed IoT Smart Grid Coordination for Industrial & Commercial DBs

In Sri Lanka, evening peak hours force utilities to burn costly, imported fossil fuels (diesel/heavy oil) to meet transient spikes in demand. GridGuard tackles this national economic and climatic challenge by flattening the demand curve through an intelligent, low-cost decentralized load-shifting architecture.

  • Decoupled Low-Cost Nodes: Intelligent ESP32 smart plugs and switches control appliances without expensive onboard power meters, keeping individual node production costs remarkably low.
  • Single DB Power Meter & ΔP Sensing: A single central PZEM node at the main distribution board measures total whole-home power. The central Raspberry Pi controller calculates step-change surges (ΔP) to identify which load was activated.
  • Smart Policy & User Override: Essential lighting loads are never delayed (fully controllable via physical buttons and web). Heavy loads (>1000W like kettles or heaters) are autonomously delayed during peak hours, with an immediate 1-tap OVERRIDE button for user freedom.
  • Bill Prediction & Automated Timers: Whole-home power tracking enables real-time monthly bill forecasting in LKR, countdown timers, and automated daily off-peak scheduling.
